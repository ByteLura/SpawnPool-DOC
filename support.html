Support & Scope

Support

Support: bytelura@gmail.com

Write to that address — it is the one support door for SpawnPool Pro, so nothing gets lost between inboxes.

What to include when you contact support

  • Your Unity version (for example, a 2022.3 LTS release or a Unity 6 release).
  • SpawnPool Pro version: 1.0.0.
  • What you did, what you expected, and what happened instead.
  • If Verify Install reported a problem, the exact per-check wording from the window's header status line.
  • If a pool's numbers look wrong, the pool's row from the dashboard — Active, Created, Peak, Cap hits, and Warnings carry most of the story.

Scope of this version

Version 1.0.0 includes:

  • Typed pools for plain C# class instances and per-prefab GameObject pools behind one small API.
  • Prewarm, growth caps with fail-safe refusal and an opt-in exceed flag, delayed despawn, and scene preloading through PoolPreloader — with no manager object to place by hand.
  • Per-pool live analytics in one Editor dashboard, with long-checkout and warning surfacing.
  • Lifecycle callbacks with a guaranteed order, FIFO reuse, and detected, counted misuse handling.
  • The Pool Playground sample and the documentation set.

Version 1 does not include the items on the Honest Limits page — no Addressables integration, no networking authority, no analytics persistence or export, no automatic pool sizing, no struct pool, and nothing render-pipeline specific. That page is part of the product's scope statement, not fine print.
